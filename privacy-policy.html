Information we may collect

When you use this website or submit a request, HVAC may collect information that you provide voluntarily. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and ZIP code.
  • The HVAC service category you select.
  • Project details you enter into the request form.
  • Communication preferences or consent confirmations.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, pages viewed and general usage data.

HVAC may also use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to remember preferences and improve website usability.

How information may be used

HVAC may use collected information for purposes related to the operation of this provider matching website, including to:

  1. Review and process your HVAC provider matching request.
  2. Help connect users with independent provider options when available.
  3. Communicate about your request or follow-up steps.
  4. Improve website performance, usability and content clarity.
  5. Maintain records related to consent, requests, website activity and legal compliance.
  6. Protect against misuse, spam, fraud or unauthorized activity.
Important matching notice Submitting a request does not guarantee that a provider will be available, that pricing will be offered, or that any provider will accept a project.

HVAC may share request information with independent providers, service partners, operational vendors or other parties involved in responding to a provider matching request.

Independent providers are not employees of HVAC. Providers may have their own privacy practices, communication methods, pricing policies and service terms. Homeowners should review provider details directly before choosing who to contact or hire.

  • Request details may be shared to help identify relevant provider options.
  • Technical data may be shared with analytics, hosting or security vendors.
  • Information may be disclosed when required by law, legal process or safety needs.
