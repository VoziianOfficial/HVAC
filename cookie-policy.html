What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites may place on your browser or device. They can help websites remember preferences, improve navigation and support basic functionality.

Similar technologies, including local storage, may store small pieces of information in your browser without using traditional cookie files.

  • Improving website usability and navigation.
  • Understanding general website performance and page activity.

  • Maintaining consistent user experience across pages.

Your cookie choices

You can accept or decline the cookie banner when it appears. You can also manage or delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings.
